Privacy Policy

Effective Sep 21, 2026

Overview

This application is a personal, non-public tool built and operated solely by the developer to publish the developer's own original video content to TikTok accounts owned and controlled by the developer. It is not offered to the public and does not collect, process, or store personal data belonging to anyone other than the developer.

Data Accessed

Through TikTok's Content Posting API, this application accesses and stores OAuth access and refresh tokens for the developer's own TikTok accounts, used solely to authorize posting video content on the developer's behalf. No data belonging to TikTok users other than the developer is accessed, collected, or processed.

Data Storage & Security

Access tokens are stored securely and are never shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party. Tokens are used only to operate this application and are deleted when an account's authorization is revoked or no longer needed.

Third Parties

This application does not share any data with third parties and does not use the data it accesses for advertising, analytics, or any purpose beyond posting the developer's own content.

Children's Privacy

This application is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.
